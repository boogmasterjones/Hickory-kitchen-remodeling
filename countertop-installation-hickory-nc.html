Countertop Installation in Hickory, NC: Process, Timeline & Cost

Countertops Updated August 2026 7 min read

Whether you're replacing dated laminate or finishing out a full kitchen remodel, knowing what actually happens during countertop installation helps you plan around it — and spot a rushed job before it happens.

Searching for countertop installation in Hickory, NC usually means you're at one of two points: you're finalizing a full kitchen remodel, or you're replacing worn, dated counters on their own. Either way, the installation process itself follows the same core steps — and understanding them helps you know what to expect, budget accurately, and avoid the corners some installers cut.

The Countertop Installation Process, Step by Step

  1. In-home measurement and templating. A installer visits your kitchen to measure precisely — often with a digital laser template — capturing exact dimensions, corners, and the locations of your sink, cooktop, and outlets.
  2. Material and slab selection. If you're choosing a natural stone like granite or marble, you'll typically visit a slab yard to pick the exact piece going into your kitchen. Quartz is chosen from manufactured samples instead.
  3. Fabrication. The countertop is cut to your exact template, edges are profiled and polished, and cutouts for sinks and cooktops are made — all done in a shop, not on-site.
  4. Old countertop removal. On installation day, your existing countertop is removed and disposed of before the new one goes in.
  5. Installation. The new countertop sections are set, leveled, and seamed together with a color-matched epoxy so seams are minimally visible.
  6. Sink, faucet, and cooktop hookup. Plumbing and cooktop connections are reinstalled, and everything is sealed and cleaned before the crew leaves.

Realistic Installation Timeline

Most homeowners are surprised to learn the installation itself is fast — it's the lead-up that takes time. Here's a typical timeline:

StageTypical Time
Initial estimate & material selection1–3 days
Templating visitScheduled within a week
Fabrication1–2 weeks
Old counter removal & installation1 day

Total time from your first call to a finished countertop is typically 2 to 3 weeks. Custom or exotic stone slabs, or a heavy shop backlog, can push that timeline slightly.

What Drives Countertop Installation Cost

Two kitchens with the same square footage can have very different countertop quotes. The variables that actually move the price:

  • Material — laminate and butcher block are the most affordable; quartz and granite are mid-range; marble and exotic stone slabs cost more.
  • Edge profile — a simple eased edge costs less than a waterfall or ogee edge.
  • Number of cutouts — sinks, cooktops, and outlet cutouts each add fabrication time.
  • Old countertop removal — nearly always included, but complexity (tile backsplashes tied into the old counter, for example) can add labor.
  • Square footage and seams — larger kitchens or islands may require additional slabs and seams.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does countertop installation take from start to finish?

From initial measurement to final installation, most projects take 2 to 3 weeks total. Fabrication takes 1–2 weeks, and installation itself is usually a single day.

Can I keep using my kitchen during countertop installation?

You'll be without countertops for roughly a day during the physical installation, since the old counters come out before the new ones go in. The rest of your kitchen stays usable throughout the weeks leading up to it.

Do I need to remove my old countertops myself?

No — removal and disposal of the old countertop is included as part of professional installation.
