Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

Common questions about remodeling a kitchen in Hickory

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Hickory, NC?

Kitchen remodeling cost depends on the size of your kitchen and the scope — a cabinet refacing with new countertops is a very different project from a full layout change. The honest answer comes from a free in-home estimate, where we measure and give you an itemized written quote with no obligation.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most full kitchen remodels in the Hickory area run a few weeks once materials are on hand, while smaller updates like cabinet refacing or a countertop swap are faster. We give you a realistic timeline up front and keep you posted as the work moves along.

Do you handle both kitchen design and construction?

Yes. We help plan the layout, choose cabinets, countertops, and finishes, and then do the build — so you have one team from concept to final walkthrough.

What kitchen remodeling services do you offer?

Full kitchen remodeling, custom cabinets and refacing, countertop installation, kitchen islands, backsplash and tile, and kitchen flooring — across Hickory and the surrounding foothills.

Is the kitchen estimate really free?

Yes — the in-home estimate and the written quote are free, with no obligation to move forward. Call (941) 327-9667 to set one up.
