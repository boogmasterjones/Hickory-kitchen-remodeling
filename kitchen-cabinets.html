Two paths, one great result

New cabinets or refacing — we'll help you choose

The right answer depends on the condition of your current cabinets, your layout, and your budget. Here's the honest difference.

Custom & Semi-Custom Cabinets

When you're changing the layout or your boxes are worn out, new cabinets give you full control over storage, style, and materials — soft-close drawers, pull-outs, and finishes chosen to match your design.

Cabinet Refacing

If your boxes are solid and the layout works, refacing swaps doors, drawer fronts, and veneers for a fresh look at a fraction of the cost and time — often finished in days, not weeks.

Kitchen Cabinet Installation & Refacing in Hickory, NC

Cabinets set the tone for the entire room, which is why so many Hickory kitchen projects start here. Whether you're after fully custom kitchen cabinets or a cost-effective cabinet refacing near me in the Catawba Valley, we measure precisely, order cabinets to spec, and hang everything level and true so drawers and doors line up the way they should.

Popular choices with Hickory homeowners include classic white and greige shaker cabinets, warm stained oak and hickory, deep navy and forest-green islands paired with lighter perimeter cabinets, and painted inset cabinetry for a more built-in, furniture-grade look. For kitchens where the boxes are still solid, refacing or refinishing is the smart, lower-waste way to modernize.

Cabinet Options We Coordinate

  • Shaker, flat-panel (slab), and inset door styles
  • Painted, stained, and thermofoil finishes
  • Soft-close hinges and full-extension drawer glides
  • Pull-out shelves, spice racks, trash pull-outs, and corner solutions
  • Island and pantry cabinetry, plus crown and trim details
  • Cabinet refacing, refinishing, and door-front replacement
