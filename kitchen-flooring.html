Kitchen Flooring Installation in Hickory, NC

The floor ties a kitchen together and takes the most punishment, so choosing the right material matters. Homeowners searching for kitchen flooring installers near me in Hickory increasingly choose luxury vinyl plank (LVP) for its waterproof core, realistic wood look, and comfort underfoot — an ideal fit for busy kitchens and open floor plans. We level the subfloor properly first, because even the best flooring telegraphs a bad subfloor.

Porcelain and ceramic tile flooring remains a durable, timeless choice, especially paired with a matching backsplash, while hardwood and engineered wood let the kitchen flow seamlessly into adjoining living and dining spaces — a look in high demand across the Catawba Valley's open-concept homes. Whatever you choose, we'll help match the flooring to your cabinets, counters, and the rest of your remodel.

Kitchen Flooring Options We Coordinate

  • Baseboards, thresholds, and flush transitions to other rooms
