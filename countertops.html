The right countertop for how you cook

New countertops are one of the highest-impact changes you can make in a kitchen — and the surface you choose affects durability, maintenance, and budget. We template precisely so seams land where they should and your counters sit flat and true.

  • Quartz — durable, low-maintenance, huge color range
  • Granite & quartzite — natural stone with unique veining
  • Marble — timeless, classic look for a statement kitchen
  • Butcher block — warm, natural wood for islands and prep areas

Kitchen Countertop Installation in Hickory, NC

Whether you're replacing dated laminate or finishing a full kitchen remodel, new countertops set the tone for the entire room. Homeowners searching for quartz countertops near me or granite countertop installation in Hickory, NC want a surface that looks great, holds up to daily cooking, and is installed without visible seams or lippage. We handle the whole process — measuring, digital templating, edge profiling, cutouts for sinks and cooktops, and precise installation.

Quartz remains the most popular choice in the Catawba Valley for its durability and near-zero maintenance, while granite and quartzite countertops appeal to homeowners who want the one-of-a-kind veining of natural stone. Marble delivers a classic, high-end look, and butcher block adds warmth to an island or coffee station. Whatever you choose, we'll help you pair the countertop with the right edge profile, backsplash, and cabinet finish.

Countertop Options We Coordinate

  • Quartz countertops in solid, marble-look, and speckled patterns
  • Granite and quartzite slabs with natural veining
  • Marble for islands, perimeters, and baking stations
  • Butcher block and solid-wood tops
  • Edge profiles — eased, beveled, bullnose, ogee, and mitered waterfall edges
  • Undermount sink cutouts, cooktop cutouts, and matching backsplashes
