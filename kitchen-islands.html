More than a countertop

An island built around how you use your kitchen

A well-designed island does real work: extra prep surface, a home for the sink or cooktop, deep drawers and pull-outs, a spot for stools, and a natural gathering place. We plan the island around traffic flow and clearances so it opens the kitchen up instead of crowding it.

  • Seating overhangs for stools and casual dining
  • Deep drawers, pull-outs, and hidden storage
  • Prep sinks, cooktops, outlets, and pendant lighting
  • Waterfall edges and two-tone island finishes

Custom Kitchen Islands in Hickory, NC

When homeowners search for a kitchen island installer near me in Hickory, they're usually after one of two things: more usable space, or a true centerpiece for an open-concept kitchen. A custom island delivers both. We size the island to your room, leaving the right walkways on every side so it feels generous rather than tight — a detail builder-grade islands often get wrong.

Islands are also where a lot of a kitchen's personality lives. A deep-green or navy island beneath a lighter perimeter, a warm butcher-block top for baking, a waterfall quartz edge, statement pendant lights, and hidden outlets all start at the design stage. If your current layout has a cramped peninsula or a wasted galley, opening it into an island is one of the most requested changes in a Hickory kitchen remodel.

Kitchen Island Features We Coordinate

  • Prep and seating islands sized to your kitchen's clearances
  • Two-tone and painted island cabinetry
  • Quartz, granite, and butcher-block island tops
  • Built-in sinks, cooktops, microwave drawers, and wine storage
  • Electrical outlets, USB ports, and pendant lighting
  • Peninsula-to-island conversions and layout changes
