Kitchen Backsplash Installation in Hickory, NC

A new backsplash is one of the most cost-effective ways to update a kitchen, which is why so many Hickory homeowners search for backsplash installation near me after new countertops or cabinets go in. The material is only part of it — the result depends on layout and craftsmanship. Our experienced tile setters dry-lay the pattern first, keep grout lines consistent, and handle the tricky cuts around windows, outlets, and range hoods so the finished wall looks seamless.

Popular choices across the Catawba Valley include timeless white subway tile, warm handmade-look ceramic, dramatic marble and natural-stone mosaics, and full-height slab backsplashes that carry the countertop material up the wall behind the range. Beyond backsplashes, we also coordinate broader kitchen tile installation, including accent walls and adjoining areas.

Backsplash & Tile Options We Coordinate

  • Subway tile — classic offset, stacked, and vertical layouts
  • Mosaic sheets — glass, marble, penny round, and hexagon
  • Herringbone, chevron, and basketweave patterns
  • Porcelain, ceramic, glass, and natural-stone tile
  • Full-height and behind-the-range slab backsplashes
  • Schluter edge trim, mitered returns, and matched grout
