Granite Countertop Company in Hickory, NC: What to Look For

Granite is one of the most requested countertop materials in the Hickory area — but the material only tells half the story. The company that measures, fabricates, and installs it determines whether you get a seamless, level counter or years of regret. Here's what to actually look for.

Why Granite Still Holds Up as a Top Countertop Choice

Granite is a natural stone, quarried and cut into slabs, which means every single piece has unique veining and mineral patterns — no two granite countertops look exactly alike. For homeowners who want a kitchen that feels distinctive rather than mass-produced, that's a real selling point. Granite is also naturally heat-resistant (you can set a hot pan directly on it without damage) and, once properly sealed, highly resistant to scratching and everyday wear.

The tradeoff is maintenance: granite is porous and needs to be sealed periodically — usually once a year, sometimes less depending on the specific stone — to resist staining from oil, wine, and acidic foods. If you want a similar look with zero sealing, quartz is worth comparing (we cover that in our countertop installation services page). But for homeowners who love natural stone and don't mind the occasional sealing, granite remains one of the best-performing materials on the market.

What Separates a Good Granite Countertop Company From a Bad One

The material is the easy part. The fabrication and installation are where quality actually gets decided. Here's what to look for when you're evaluating a granite countertop company near me:

  • They template your kitchen digitally or with physical templates before cutting — never eyeball measurements from a tape measure alone. A templated cut is what makes seams land in the least visible spots and cutouts fit sinks and cooktops precisely.
  • They let you see and approve the actual slab before it's cut, not just a sample chip. Granite varies significantly slab to slab, and you should know exactly what pattern is going into your kitchen.
  • They explain edge profile options — eased, bullnose, ogee, or a beveled edge — and how each affects both look and price.
  • They provide a written, itemized quote before any work begins, not a verbal ballpark that changes once the job starts.
  • They handle sink and cooktop cutouts as part of the installation, with clean, polished edges around every cutout — not just the visible perimeter.

Granite vs. Quartz: A Quick Comparison

Homeowners comparing granite countertops to quartz in Hickory usually land on one of these two, so here's the honest breakdown:

  • Maintenance: Granite needs annual sealing; quartz needs none.
  • Look: Granite is 100% natural with unique veining; quartz is engineered and more color-consistent.
  • Heat resistance: Granite handles direct heat better than quartz.
  • Cost: Mid-grade granite and quartz are often comparably priced; premium granite slabs can run higher than quartz.
A granite countertop is only as good as the crew that templates, cuts, and installs it. Ask to see photos of finished seams before you commit — that's the single best indicator of quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does granite cost per square foot installed in Hickory, NC?

Most granite countertop installations in the area run roughly $50 to $100 per square foot installed, depending on the grade of stone, edge profile, and cutouts needed. A free in-home measurement is the only way to get an exact number.

Is granite still a good choice compared to quartz?

Yes — granite remains a strong choice if you want the one-of-a-kind look of natural stone and don't mind sealing it every year or two. Quartz trades that natural variation for near-zero maintenance.

How long does granite countertop installation take?

Once your slab is selected and templated, fabrication typically takes 1–2 weeks, with installation completed in a single day for most kitchens.
